Translation: Khatir Se Ya Lihaaz Se — Daagh Dehlvi

A very romantic poem by Daagh Dehlvi, sung by Shumona Roy Biswas. The first couplet's a killer :D

Khaatir se ya lihaaz se mein maan to gaya
Jhoothi kasam se aapka eemaan to gaya

I agreed reluctantly out of courtesy,
But now you have lost my trust because of your false promise

Dil leke muft kehte hein kuch kaam ka nahin
Ulti shikaayaten huin, ehsaan to gaya

She took my heart and then says that it is useless,
She gives me complains instead of thankfulness

*Go *naamaabar se khush na hua par hazaar shukr
Mujhko vo mere naam se pehchaan to gaya

Even though she wasn't happy to see the messenger
At least, she recognized me by my name in the message

Hosh-o-havaas-o-*taab-o-*tavan "Daagh" ja chuke
Ab ham bhi jaane waale hein saamaan to gaya

My consciousness, patience and strength are already gone,
Now that all my things have left me, I too am about to depart

Go - Although
Namaabar - messenger
Taab - Patience
Tavaan - Strength

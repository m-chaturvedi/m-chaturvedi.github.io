Another gem by Beghum Akhtar. Written by Ali Ahmed Jalili.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YcmFbulK2DY

<br><br>
<i>Khushi ne mujhko thukraya hai, dard-o-gham ne paala hai<br>
Gulon ne be-rukhi ki hai, toh kaanton ne sambhala hai</i>

Happiness deserted me while pain accommodated,<br>
Flowers snubbed me while the thorns supported

<i>Mohabbat mein khayaal-e-sahil-o-manzil hai naadaani,<br>
Jo in raahon mein lut jaaye, wahi taqdeer waala hai</i>

Looking for shore in the ocean of love is immaturity,<br>
Lucky are those who drown during their journey

<i>Charagan kar ke dil behla rahe ho kya jahan waalon,<br>
Andhera laakh roshan ho, ujaala phir ujaala hai</i>

Why are people trying to ward off darkness (sorrow) by lighting candles?<br>
There's only so much artificial lighting can do when compared to natural light

<i>Kinaaron se mujhe ae naakhudaaon door hi rakhna<br>
Wahan lekar chalo toofan jahan se uthne waala hai</i>

Keep me (my ship) away from the safe shores, dear Captain (Naakhuda)<br>
Take me where a storm is about to rise

<i>Nasheman hi ke lut jaane ka gham hota to kya gham tha<br>
Yahan toh bechne waale ne, gulshan bech daala hai</i>

If only I was sad for just having my nest ripped off,<br>
The sellers here, have sold the whole garden.

--- Ali Ahmed Jalili

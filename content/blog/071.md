An attempt at a literal translation.
Sung by Farida Khanum.

<b><i>Yeh kya ke ek jahan ko karo *vaqf-e-*iztiraab,<br>
Yeh kya ke ek dil ko *shakeba na kar sako!</i></b>

You can make the whole world feel discontent with desire for you,<br>
But you can't make even one heart content!<br>

[I think that there are three things being said here:
1. A compliment, that you're so beautiful that the whole world squirms in desire for you :).
2. A complaint that you can't even make one person content.
3. An insinuation that if you be kindly towards me, make my heart content, you won't be considered so cruel ;-)]

<i>Aisa na ho ye dard bane dard-e-la-dawa,<br>
Aisa na ho ke tum bhi *madawa na kar sako<br></i>

I hope this pain doesn't become so uncurable,<br>
That even you are not able to do anything about it<br>

<i>Shayad tumhe bhi chain na aaye mere bagair<br>
Shayad ye baat tum bhi *gavaaran na kar sako<br></i>

Perhaps you too would terribly miss me,<br>
Perhaps you too would not be able to bear it!<br>
[ This seems like [a Chinese curse.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_you_live_in_interesting_times) ]


<i>Allah kare jahan ko meri yaad bhool jaaye,<br>
Allah kare ke tum kabhi aisa na kar sako<br></i>

I pray that the whole world forgets me,<br>
But God doesn't ever let you do so<br>

<i>Kya jaane phir sitam bhi *mayassar hon ya na hon,<br>
Kya jaane ye karam bhi karo ya na kar sako!<br></i>

Who knows if even these tyrannies would be possible in the future!<br>
Perhaps the future would be bereft of even these favors<br>

---- Sufi Tabassum
Rekhta page

*vaqf* : engrossed<br>
*iztiraab* : restlessness<br>
*shakeba* : content/patient<br>
*gavaaran* : bearable/tolerable<br>
*madawa* : cure<br>
*mayassar* : possible<br>

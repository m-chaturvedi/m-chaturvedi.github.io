A wonderful, wonderful ghazal by [Suroor Barabankvi](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suroor_Barabankvi) sung delightfully by Mehnaz Begum and a futile attempt at translation.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgzCJwv0Z5s

<i>Tu *uroos-e-shaam-e-khayal bhi, tu *jamaal-e-ru-e-sahar bhi hai,<br>
Ye zaroor hai, ke *ba i hamah mera *ehtimaam-e-nazar bhi hai</i>

You’re the topic of the evenings of mentation, You’re the soul of the splendor of the morning’s sun,<br>
Nevertheless, I realize that some of it could just be my imagination

<i>Ye mera naseeb hai, humnasheen, sar-e-raah bhi na mile kahin,<br>
Wahi mera *jaada-e-justjoo, wahi unki raah-guzar bhi hai</i>

It was in my fate, that Our paths never crossed,<br>
Even when the road I roam about on is the one which leads to Her home

<b><i>Tere *qurb ne jo badha diye, kabhi mit sake na wo faasle,<br>
Vahi paanv hein, vahi *aable, vahi apna *zauq-e-safar bhi hai</i></b>

Your brief nearness to me did increase the distances between us, [i.e. aggravate my longing for Her?] which I could never traverse,<br>
Although my feet's strength, blisters and the taste for wandering never faded

<i>Bahaazar *daanish-o-*aagahi, meri *maslehat hai abhi yahi,<br>
Mein *aseer-e-zulmat-e-shab sahi, meri *dastaras mein sahar bhi hai</i>

I’m not very knowledgeable, but I know this,<br>
Although now I’m suffering in darkness, light is also within my reach

--- Suroor Barabankvi


<i>uroos</i> = bride<br>
<i>jamaal</i> = beauty<br>
<i>ba i hamah</i> = nevertheless<br>
<i>ehtimaam</i> = arrangement<br>
<i>jaada</i> = path<br>
<i>qurb</i> = nearness<br>
<i>aable</i> = blisters <br>
<i>zauq</i> = taste<br>
<i>daanish</i> = knowledge<br>
<i>aagahi</i> = awareness<br>
<i>maslehat</i> = understanding<br>
<i>aseer</i> = captive<br>
<i>dastaras</i> = reach

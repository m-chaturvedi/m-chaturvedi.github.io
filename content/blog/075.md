A very romantic poem by Daagh Dehlvi, sung by Shumona Roy Biswas.
The first couplet's a killer :D

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IpZA-gz2SbY

<i>Khaatir se ya lihaaz se mein maan to gaya<br>
Jhoothi kasam se aapka eemaan to gaya</i>

I agreed reluctantly out of courtesy,<br>
But now you have lost my trust because of your false promise

<i>Dil leke muft kehte hein kuch kaam ka nahin<br>
Ulti shikaayaten huin, ehsaan to gaya</i>

She took my heart and then says that it is useless,<br>
She gives me complains instead of thankfulness

<i>*Go *naamaabar se khush na hua par hazaar shukr<br>
Mujhko vo mere naam se pehchaan to gaya</i>

Even though she wasn't happy to see the messenger<br>
At least, she recognized me by my name in the message

<i>Hosh-o-havaas-o-*taab-o-*tavan "Daagh" ja chuke<br>
Ab ham bhi jaane waale hein saamaan to gaya</i>

My consciousness, patience and strength are already gone,<br>
Now that all my things have left me, I too am about to depart

<i>Go</i> - Although<br>
<i>Namaabar</i> - messenger<br>
<i>Taab</i> - Patience<br>
<i>Tavaan</i> - Strength<br>
